# Voice selection: Libyan Sawti broad audition

The target is **consistent intelligible Libyan pronunciation across the entire built-in vocabulary**, rather than solely one correct G in one word.

- Test ten accessible Arabic Neural voices (Libyan female/male, Egyptian female/male, Iraqi female/male, Tunisian female/male, Saudi female/male) with **19 historically hard-G** items and **9 normal** Libyan vocabulary controls, using a 280-clip listening archive.
- Egyptian ج is only an experiment. In earlier user feedback, `ar-EG-SalmaNeural` + ج was closest for `action.sit`, but other words occasionally made J rather than G. Thus no cross-word accuracy is assumed.
- Compare (1) actual hard /ɡ/ consistently, (2) other consonants and vowels, (3) regional sounding accent (4) timing, (5) comprehensibility for aphasia users. Reject readings with omitted consonants or changed meaning.
- If no single voice meets these, allow an **audited voice choice per card** (stable choice recorded with its manifest), or use a provider with reliable IPA/phoneme control and compatible Arabic voice. Never simply swap voices globally, and never accept mixed voices without user approval.
- All source data, 2,399 production audio clips and the default Libyan `ar-LY-ImanNeural` remain unchanged. The comparison batch is entirely separate.

## Listening preference — 2026-11-16

After comparing the 10-voice bake-off pack, user selected **Egyptian Shakir** (`ar-EG-ShakirNeural`) as their preferred candidate for Libyan words. This is a **voice preference**, not yet an exhaustive per-word phonetic approval or permission to replace production audio without validating the 271 Libyan entries, especially hard /ɡ/ vs /dʒ/ and general Libyan prosody. Carry this voice forward as the first candidate in follow-up tests and preserve the original v1.6.2 production voice pack until explicitly approved for release.
