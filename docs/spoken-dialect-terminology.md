# Sawti – spoken vocabulary by dialect

The nine variants in `DialectCatalog.kt` are ordered:
STANDARD, LIBYAN, SAUDI, EGYPTIAN, SYRIAN, IRAQI, TUNISIAN, MOROCCAN, ALGERIAN.

| Meaning | Libyan | Tunisian | Algerian | Egyptian | Syrian | Iraqi | Saudi | Moroccan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggs | دحي | عظام | ولاد الجاج | بيض | بيض | بيض | بيض | بيض |
| Socks | شخاشير | كلسيطة | تقاشر | شرابات | جرابات | جواريب | دلاغات | تقاشر |
| Water | إمّيه | ما | الما | مية | مي | ماي | ماي | الما |

Daily *water requests* are complete dialect-dependent phrases; the independent water card uses the noun above.

Other commonly used regional forms include مقرونة، جلبانة، شفلور، دنجال، مطيشة،
كوباية، جنطة، جاكيطة، قوري، وغيرها. Unmapped labels are left intact when
the word itself is common across regions. This avoids inventing regional words.

All shared built-in photos are keyed by the original persisted WordItem.semanticKey
and are never resolved from the translated caption. Custom/family entries are
not auto-translated. Country-specific cuisine vocabularies are separately managed
and will receive their own photos in a future update.

These spellings are practical readable dialect forms, not TTS engine guarantees.
